Perigee

A slow drift, flagged about 8 nights in. Nothing worn, nothing to remember.

ISS crews average about 6.1 hours of sleep, and circadian misalignment costs close to another hour. Perigee keeps a personal baseline through those short nights, so a crew member and their flight surgeon see change while it is still small.
