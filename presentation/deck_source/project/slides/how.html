How Perigee works, every night

One small receiver by the bunk. Four steps between lights-out and the morning glance.

Sense

Wi-Fi channel state gives breathing, presence and movement, every 30 s.

Learn

Each night joins a personal 28-night baseline, if it was calm and long enough.

Detect

Robust z and a CUSUM memory turn small shifts into Watch, then Alert.

Speak

Plain flags in the person’s own tone, with weekly check-ins and a companion.

Safety first: a check-in that needs support opens the safety screen before anything else is scored

Trace the arc left to right: sense, learn, detect, speak. Everything after "sense" is math we show next. The one rule that sits above the arc: if a check-in signals the person needs support, the safety screen comes first and nothing else runs.
