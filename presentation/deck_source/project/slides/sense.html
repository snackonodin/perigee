From a Wi-Fi channel to a breath rate

Every 30 seconds, one 60-second window goes through three transformations.

Raw channel. Amplitudes from 20 subcarriers, about 100 packets a second.

Breathing, filtered. 0.1–0.7 Hz keeps 6–42 breaths a minute. Count the peaks: 16.2.

Spectrum. The peak inside the band reads 16.0. Two methods, one answer.

If the two counts disagree by more than 3 breaths a minute, the window is thrown away.

Watch the wave draw and the four peaks get counted, then the spectrum peak lands inside the shaded breathing band. The values 16.2 and 16.0 are illustrative of one window. The rule is real: spectral peak and peak count must agree within 3 br/min and the peak-to-noise ratio must clear 3 dB, or the window is discarded. Motion (robust z of variance at least 4) and an empty room (variance under a tenth of the session median) also drop windows.
